The trip to Sunshine Hospital

Without a car, the trip from Melton often takes a bus, two trains and a walk. Then you need to get home again on the same day.

One common way to go

  1. Home in Melton
  2. Walk to the bus stop about 10 min Middle value for Melton areas.
  3. Bus stop
  4. Bus A local bus to the railway station.
  5. Railway station

    For example Melton or Cobblebank.

  6. V/Line train The regional train towards the city.
  7. Sunshine station

    Change trains.

  8. Metro train, Sunbury line A short ride towards Sunbury.
  9. Ginifer station

    The station closest to the hospital.

  10. Walk From the station to the hospital entrance.
  11. Sunshine Hospital

Why the trip home matters

A hospital visit only works if you can also get home on the same day. If there is no bus or train home, the visit does not work, even when the trip there is easy. So we always look at both trips together.

Leaving hospital at 6 pm 391 of 402 areas have a trip home
Leaving hospital at 8:30 pm 222 of 402 areas have a trip home
Trip home by public transport No trip home

At 8:30 pm, 180 areas have no trip home. This includes 21 of the 31 priority areas. For 20 priority areas, the last trip home leaves the hospital at 8:26 pm.

This is why the explorer shows two times: leaving hospital at 6 pm and at 8:30 pm.
