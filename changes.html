Four changes, compared with the Sept 2026 timetable

Each change uses routes that already exist. None adds a new route or stop. We compare each one with the Sept 2026 timetable, at two times: leaving hospital at 6 pm and at 8:30 pm.

  • More frequent buses
  • Bus–train timing
  • Later buses
  • Cobblebank site

The four changes help different areas

A priority area has a long trip to hospital AND many homes without a car. Only more frequent buses and the Cobblebank site make some areas stop being priority areas.

More frequent buses

One extra bus trip halfway between each pair of existing trips.

The extra trips run on the same bus routes and stop at the same stops. More buses mean shorter waits for some people.

The improvement disappears if you miss one connection

We tested a missed first connection, with a delay of 10 or 20 minutes. Then the improvement drops to 0 areas.

Bus–train timing

Bus trip times shifted to meet V/Line trains.

Bus times are moved so buses arrive at the station in time for a V/Line train. This is done for all Melton buses that feed the stations.

Some trips get shorter, others get longer

Out of 380 areas compared at 6 pm, 68 round trips get shorter and 77 get longer.

Later buses

Extra late buses on existing routes, leaving as late as 10 pm.

At 6 pm nothing changes: people are home before the extra buses run. The change shows up later in the evening, when, on the Sept 2026 timetable, many areas have no trip home.

Every new trip home depends on one bus

Every new trip home uses the same extra bus: route 456, leaving its first stop at 9:48 pm.

Cobblebank site

The Cobblebank site is tested with the Sept 2026 buses and trains, not a future timetable.

A new hospital is planned at Cobblebank, inside the City of Melton. We move the trip's end point to that site. The buses and trains stay as they are on the Sept 2026 timetable, because no future timetable is public.

Late at night, many trips get longer

At 8:30 pm, 181 areas have a trip home both on the Sept 2026 timetable and with the Cobblebank site. Of these, 121 get longer and 60 get shorter.
