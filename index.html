Can Melton residents without a car get to hospital and home again?

  • 31 priority areas today
  • about 510 homes with no car in those areas
  • 168 min typical full round trip to Sunshine Hospital and home

We looked at the trip from every part of the City of Melton to Sunshine Hospital by bus and train. The trip must work both ways on the same day. Then we tested four changes to see which one helps the most people.

What this means

In our tests, a hospital site closer to Melton helped the most areas at 6 pm. But late at night, many trips from that site get longer.

Changes to bus services can still help. One extra late bus gives 19 priority areas a way home after an evening visit.

These gains are fragile. The late-bus gain depends on that one bus. The gain from more frequent buses is lost if a traveller misses one connection.

So better hospital access needs more than extra services. People also need connections they can rely on, all the way home.
